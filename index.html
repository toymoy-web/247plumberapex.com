Before Help Arrives

What to Do in a Plumbing Emergency

  • Shut off the main water valve if a pipe has burst or a fixture is actively leaking.
  • Switch off the water heater's power or gas supply if it's leaking near electrical outlets or a pilot light.
  • Move valuables and electronics away from standing water.
  • Photograph visible damage before cleanup for any insurance claim.
  • Avoid running a garbage disposal or dishwasher if a kitchen drain is backed up.

These steps limit damage while a plumber is on the way. They don't replace calling right away for anything involving a gas smell, sewage backup, or standing water near electrical outlets.
