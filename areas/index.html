Coverage Across Apex

Apex spans three ZIP codes, 27502, 27523, and 27539, and the neighborhoods below make up the core of the standard dispatch area. Each one has a genuinely different housing history, from Green Level's 1800s crossroads to Salem's newer quarter-acre lots, which is part of why the plumbing issues that come up in one town don't always match another.

A town's construction era is usually the strongest predictor of what a service call there looks like. Older, established neighborhoods with mature trees tend to see more root-intrusion drain issues and aging copper supply lines, while newer construction corridors generate more inspection and hookup work alongside the occasional builder-grade fixture repair.
