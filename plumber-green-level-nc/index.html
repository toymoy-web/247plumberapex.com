Old and New in Green Level

Green Level is a genuinely split call area: a handful of homes tied to the historic 1800s crossroads, recognized on the National Register of Historic Places, sitting alongside brand-new construction from builders like M/I Homes along Green Level West Road. Those two housing populations need almost opposite kinds of plumbing work.

The older homes near the historic crossroads may still run original galvanized or early copper lines, while new-construction sections use PEX and current fittings throughout, which is why a water damage call here can mean very different things depending on which side of the neighborhood it's coming from. All new-construction plumbing work is verified against current Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) requirements as adopted under North Carolina's plumbing code before a section is signed off.

The crossroads itself dates back to around 1800 as a stop along the old Raleigh-to-Pittsboro road, and that history is part of why the immediate area has a genuine mix of very old and very new plumbing systems within a short drive of each other, unlike most Apex neighborhoods built in a single era.
