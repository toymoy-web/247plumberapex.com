Fuquay-Varina, South of Apex on NC-55

Fuquay-Varina sits south of Apex along NC-55, a bit farther out than Holly Springs but still within reasonable dispatch range for an emergency call. The town grew from two historic communities, Fuquay Springs and Varina, that merged in 1963, and that history is still visible in the Fuquay Springs Historic District downtown.

Like much of the fast-growing Triangle, newer construction has filled in steadily around that historic core, including the Sunset Lake neighborhood that straddles the Fuquay-Varina and Holly Springs border.
