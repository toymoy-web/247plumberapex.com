Holly Springs, Just South of Apex

Holly Springs sits directly south of Apex along US-1 and NC-55, close enough that many residents already consider Apex part of their everyday shopping and service area. The town has grown quickly over the past two decades, from a small historic crossroads into one of the larger towns in this part of Wake County.

That growth means a real mix of housing stock: established neighborhoods like Sunset Ridge near Devil's Ridge Golf Club, lakefront homes in Sunset Lake Village, and newer construction filling in closer to the historic downtown district.

What Holly Springs Homeowners Call About

Lakefront homes in Sunset Lake Village and Sunset Lake Estates sit close to the 125-acre lake the neighborhood is named for, which means drainage and sump pump questions come up more often here than in Holly Springs neighborhoods set back from the water.

Sunset Ridge, built around Devil's Ridge Golf Club, and the newer sections near Bass Lake Park see a more typical mix of water heater and fixture calls, while homes closer to the historic downtown core, where some of the town's oldest structures still stand, occasionally turn up original plumbing worth a full evaluation.
