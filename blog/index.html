What This Blog Covers

Posts here focus on the plumbing questions that come up most often across Apex homes: how long a water heater should realistically last given Wake County's water hardness, what a recurring drain clog usually means, and what to check before assuming a leak needs a professional versus a simple fix.

New posts are added as they're written and researched for this specific market, rather than generic plumbing advice that could apply anywhere. Check back, or call directly with a specific question in the meantime.
